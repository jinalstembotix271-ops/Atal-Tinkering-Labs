Basic Electronics – ATL

Voltage

Voltage is the electrical pressure that pushes electrons through a circuit.

Unit: Volt (V)

Electric Current

Current is the flow of electric charge (electrons) in a circuit.

Unit: Ampere (A)

Basic Electronic Components

Resistor – Limits current

Unit: Ohm (Ω)

Capacitor – Stores charge

Unit: Farad (F)

LED – Emits light

Ohm’s Law

Ohm’s Law shows the relationship between Voltage, Current, and Resistance.

Formula: V = I × R
