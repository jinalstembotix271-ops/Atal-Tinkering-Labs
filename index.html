Welcome to Atal Tinkering Labs (ATLs)

Atal Tinkering Labs (ATLs) are innovation workspaces established in schools across India under the Atal Innovation Mission (AIM) by NITI Aayog. ATLs encourage students to develop curiosity, creativity, and problem-solving skills through hands-on learning and experimentation.

This platform provides structured learning resources, practical activities, and project ideas to help students explore emerging technologies and transform ideas into innovations.

Learning Domains at ATL

🔌 Basic Electronics

Introduction to electronic components, current, voltage, resistance, and simple circuits.

⚡ Intermediate Electronics

Understanding sensors, transistors, ICs, and circuit design with real-world applications.

🚀 Advanced Electronics

Advanced topics such as microcontrollers, automation, robotics, and embedded systems.

🖨️ 3D Printing

Design thinking, 3D modeling, and rapid prototyping using 3D printers.

🧩 Blix Kit

Hands-on mechanical construction and engineering concepts using Blix educational kits.

🔭 Telescope

Basic astronomy concepts, sky observation, and understanding celestial objects.

🔬 Microscope

Exploring the microscopic world through biological and material science experiments.

💡 Projects

Student-driven ATL projects focusing on real-life problem solving and innovation.

🌱 Innovation Ideas

Idea generation, design thinking, and innovation challenges.

🏆 Science Fair Projects

Guidance and ideas for national and international science exhibitions.

🚁 Drone Technology

Understanding drone components, flight principles, and programming basics.

🌐 Internet of Things (IoT)

Connecting devices using sensors and cloud platforms.

🧑‍💻 Scratch Coding

Block-based programming to introduce coding fundamentals.
