Welcome to Atal Tinkering Labs (ATLs)

Atal Tinkering Labs (ATLs) are innovation workspaces established in schools across India under the Atal Innovation Mission (AIM) by NITI Aayog. ATLs encourage students to develop curiosity, creativity, and problem-solving skills through hands-on learning and experimentation.

This platform provides structured learning resources, practical activities, and project ideas to help students explore emerging technologies and transform ideas into innovations.

Learning Domains at ATL

Introduction to Electronics

Electronics is the study of electrical circuits that involve active electrical components such as resistors, capacitors, diodes, and transistors.

Electronic Components

Basic components include Resistors, Capacitors, Diodes, LEDs, Batteries, Switches, and Breadboards.

Ohm’s Law

Ohm’s Law states that Voltage (V) = Current (I) × Resistance (R).

Learn about Sensors, Transistors, Integrated Circuits (ICs), and practical circuit design techniques.

Explore Microcontrollers, Robotics, Automation, Embedded Systems, and real-world innovation projects.

Learn 3D design, modeling, slicing, and rapid prototyping using 3D printers.
